## Terminology
- Series title → «Los sistemas que construimos, los sistemas que habitamos», identical in all five `seriesPart.title` and the same as the current Spanish edition, so the series keeps one name.
- New titles (slugs unchanged): essay «Cuando el fallo es la forma del sistema»; Part 1 «La misión no manda. Manda la bañera.»; Part 2 «Lo que la métrica no ve sale gratis»; Part 3 «Apretar funciona, hasta que llega la factura»; Part 4 «El pensamiento sistémico explica, pero no adivina»; Part 5 «Tus métricas reescriben tus objetivos sin avisar».
- systems thinking → pensamiento sistémico; stock → stock («el stock, o acumulación» on first mention, Part 1); flow → flujo; inflow/outflow → entrada/salida, also inside the Part 1 code blocks so text and formula match.
- boundary → **frontera** (Parts 2, 4, 5: what a metric or model draws around itself); fence → **valla**; invisible fences → «vallas invisibles». In the essay, "boundaries" between work units → «límites», which is what a Spanish engineer would say.
- feedback loop → bucle de retroalimentación; reinforcing loop → bucle de refuerzo; delay → retraso; leverage point → punto de apalancamiento (glossed once in Part 2, «los llamados puntos de apalancamiento», since only the English essay title appears there); paradigm → paradigma.
- target → «meta» (numérica); goal → «objetivo». The distinction carries the Part 2 argument ("asking a parameter to do the work of a goal").
- capability → **capacidad** (trampa de la capacidad, stock de capacidad); capacity (time available) → «tiempo» or «margen», to avoid «capacidad para la capacidad». capability work → «trabajo que refuerza la capacidad», then «trabajo de capacidad».
- Loop labels: Work Harder → Esforzarse más; Work Smarter → Trabajar mejor; Shortcuts → Atajos; Reinvestment → Reinversión. Codes B1–B3, R1 and signs unchanged; diagram text translated. The HPA formula stays verbatim (Kubernetes notation).
- better-before-worse / worse-before-better → «mejor antes que peor» / «peor antes que mejor».
- Self-Confirming Attribution Error → error de atribución autoconfirmado (English in parentheses); fundamental attribution error → error fundamental de atribución; policy resistance → resistencia a las políticas (*policy resistance*); overshoot → sobrecorrección (*overshoot*); flapping kept as *flapping*.
- toil → *toil*, glossed «el trabajo operativo repetitivo» on first mention in Part 3 and again in Part 5 (it can be read on its own). The English does not gloss it; this follows the style guide's terminology table.
- throughput → rendimiento (*throughput* on first mention, Part 2).
- DORA metrics: tiempo de entrega de los cambios; frecuencia de despliegue; tiempo de recuperación tras un despliegue fallido; tasa de fallos de los cambios; tasa de retrabajo de los despliegues.
- game/gaming → manipular / manipulación (metrics); cheating → hacer trampas (teachers, students).
- pre-registration → «registro previo»; "pre-registered date" → «fecha registrada de antemano».
- "Part N" → «la N.ª entrega» written out («la tercera entrega»).
- proficiency counts (Part 2) → «el número de alumnos que alcanzan el nivel exigido» (no established Spanish term); the paper's title is kept in English with a rough gloss.
- Greek works: Spanish titles in running text (*Política*, *República*, *Ética a Nicómaco*); author names Jenofonte/Aristóteles/Platón in the text, English titles and names kept in References as instructed.
- Frontmatter: the YAML comment after `editorialDate` was dropped (value 2026-10-26 unchanged, matching the new originals; the current Spanish posts still carry 2026-11-02). The essay original has no `draft` field; `draft: true` added as instructed.
- Typography: decimal comma (3,7; 16,1; 2,45; 0,1); 100.000, 60.000, 80.000 but 4000; «36 %», «4-5 %».

## Open questions
- «Warden» (Part 1): rendered «un cargo público, el «Warden» de la traducción inglesa». The Greek term is *paidonomos*; the author may prefer «paidónomo».
- Part 4 References, "a sympathetic insider" → «alguien de dentro que simpatiza con la disciplina»: «con la disciplina» makes explicit what the English leaves implicit.
- "All models are wrong" → «Todos los modelos son erróneos», the usual Spanish form of Box's aphorism.
- DORA's "speed and stability are not tradeoffs" → «la velocidad y la estabilidad no son una disyuntiva». Natural, but less technical than "trade-off".
- Part 5, "The accounts Repenning and Sterman report describe a dip that was planned for, including BP Lima's…" → «describen baches previstos de antemano, como la subida… de BP Lima». The plural reads better; the English singular "a dip… including" is ambiguous in the same way.
- Part 5, "Improvement effort leads capability change by months…" was recast with capability change as the subject («el cambio en la capacidad llega meses después del esfuerzo de mejora»). Same meaning, different order.
- Toil gloss added in Parts 3 and 5 (see Terminology). The English has none; drop it if the author prefers.
- Essay title «Cuando el fallo es la forma del sistema» stays close to the English, because the English already reads well in Spanish. The Part 3 title drops "today" («Apretar funciona, hasta que llega la factura»); the timing is carried by «hasta que».
